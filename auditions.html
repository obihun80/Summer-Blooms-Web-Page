Commitment and Fit

We rehearse weekly in Moorhead during the academic year. This may be a good fit for singers who:

  • have strong pitch independence and ensemble awareness
  • enjoy chamber singing and detailed musical work
  • are comfortable carrying an independent part
  • want meaningful vocal involvement in a focused, collaborative setting

Ensemble Expectations

Summer Blooms works best when singers are reliable, prepared, and engaged. Because we are a small ensemble, each voice matters, and consistent attendance is important.

We value thoughtful musicians who are open to feedback, responsive in rehearsal, and willing to collaborate toward a shared musical interpretation.
